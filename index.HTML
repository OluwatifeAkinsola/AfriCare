Transforming Patient Engagement Across Africa

AfriCare is a digital health platform that connects patients, healthcare providers, and community health workers through accessible mobile technology.

Learn More Our Mission

The Healthcare Access Gap

Africa’s healthcare systems are overburdened, leading to preventable deaths and inefficient resource use.

Overcrowded Facilities

Long wait times and limited capacity force patients to delay or forgo care. AfriCare reduces strain through digital triage and appointment scheduling.

Poor Patient Engagement

Lack of follow‑up and health literacy leads to missed appointments and untreated chronic conditions. AfriCare’s reminders and local‑language support keep patients on track.

Our Solution

Technology that works for everyone – from feature phones to smartphones.

Omnichannel Reach

USSD, WhatsApp, and voice support in Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, and Pidgin. No internet required.

Smart Reminders

Reduce no‑show rates by 40% and improve chronic disease management with automated reminders.

CareCredit Micro‑loans

Affordable health financing through mobile money, paid in three instalments.

Revenue Model

Seven diversified streams ensure long‑term sustainability.

Hospital Subscriptions

Tiered monthly plans for clinics and large hospitals.

Transaction Commissions

15% on telemedicine and diagnostics bookings.

Micro‑loan Interest

8% on CareCredit health loans.

Government Contracts

Bids through the Basic Health Care Provision Fund.

Grants & Philanthropy

Partnerships with global health foundations.

Data Insights

Anonymised population health analytics for policy makers.

Freemium Upgrades

Premium tools for community health workers.

Competitive Advantage
